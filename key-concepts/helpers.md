# Helpers

Helpers are collections of useful, stand-alone functions, typically loaded with `Factory::helper()`. Many helpers are autoloaded.

The helpers you will use most often in admin views are the [form field](form-fields.md) family (`form_field()`, `form_field_boolean()`, `form_field_dropdown()`, …) from `nails/common`. CDN, CMS, and Admin add a few more for object pickers, widget editors, and dynamic tables.
